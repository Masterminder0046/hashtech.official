How long does a brand + website project take?

The standard process is 15 days from discovery to launch for an agreed project scope. Timely content and feedback help keep everything moving.

Can you build a website just for my coaching institute?

Yes. I can plan pages for courses, admissions, faculty information and enquiries, then design a mobile-friendly website around your institute's needs.

Do you work with businesses outside your city?

Yes. Hash_Tech works remotely with clients across India through calls, shared feedback and clear milestones.

Can you also build student or booking dashboards?

Yes. I develop custom web applications such as batch, student, booking and fee-management dashboards. Scope and timelines are discussed separately.

What happens after the website goes live?

Brand + website projects include two revision rounds and seven days of free post-launch support. We can discuss ongoing updates separately.
